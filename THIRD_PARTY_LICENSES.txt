FabMesh bundles and/or depends on the following third-party software and model
weights. Each component is distributed under its own license. Full license
texts are available at the URLs listed below. This document is provided to
satisfy the attribution requirements of those licenses.

FabMesh's shipping build (see the `files` / `extraResources` sections of
package.json) redistributes NO third-party 3D assets, character models,
textures, or pre-recorded animation clips. All bundled rig templates under
`scripts/rig_templates/` are CC0, authored by the FabMesh team, and the
Puppeteer rig backend is bundled WITHOUT its `animation/` directory.

IMPORTANT — motion library: the desktop app contains code
(`src/main/animation.js`) that can load an external FBX motion library from
`external/apovivor/1_Source/` or `c:/tmp/apovivor_fbx/`. That library is a
DEV-ONLY, out-of-tree resource and is deliberately NOT included in the
packaged build. It MUST NOT be redistributed: the FBX motions are of
Mixamo / Rokoko / apovivor provenance whose licenses prohibit redistribution
of the raw motion assets. Do not add `external/apovivor` (or any equivalent
motion pack) to `extraResources`/`files` until each clip's provenance and
redistribution rights have been individually cleared and documented here.
