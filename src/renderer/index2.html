Installation

        

          
Reconfigure MyFabmesh.AI

          
Re-run the first-time setup wizard to change install mode (Lite / Standard / Full) or re-download a missing model. Your projects and generated meshes are kept.

          

            
          

        

        

          
Uninstall MyFabmesh.AI

          
Remove MyFabmesh.AI from your computer. You'll be asked whether to also delete the AI models (~17 GB) and your settings. Your generated meshes folder is never touched.
