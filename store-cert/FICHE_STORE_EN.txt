===============================================================
 1. DESCRIPTION   (field "Description", required, max 10 000 chars)
===============================================================

MyFabmesh.AI turns a short description or a single image into a textured
3D model, ready for your game engine.

Describe what you want — "an engraved knight helmet" — or start from an
image you already have. The application generates the reference image,
converts it into a textured 3D mesh, then can add a skeleton and animate
it. Export to GLB, FBX and OBJ, usable directly in Unreal Engine, Unity
or Blender.

TWO WAYS TO USE IT

· With an NVIDIA graphics card: everything runs on your machine. No
  limit, no subscription, your creations never leave your computer. The
  AI models (about 15 to 22 GB depending on the install mode) are
  downloaded on first launch.

· Without an NVIDIA card: the application switches to our cloud service.
  An assistant detects it on first start and guides you. The cloud runs
  on credits; 15 credits are offered when you create an account, enough
  to produce your first models without paying anything.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

· Generate a reference image from a description
· Turn an image into a textured 3D mesh
· Retouch the image before going 3D: background removal, local
  inpainting, upscaling, automatic back view
· Add a skeleton automatically and animate the result
· Rework the mesh: smoothing, decimation, recentering, UVs
· Export to GLB, FBX and OBJ
· Track your projects and go back to any earlier version

AI-GENERATED CONTENT

Images and models produced by this application are generated by AI. A
report button is always present in the top bar and under every generated
image: it lets you flag inappropriate content, with or without an
account, even offline. A content filter blocks sexual, violent or illegal
requests; in the Microsoft Store version this filter cannot be turned
off.

PURCHASES

Nothing is sold inside the application. Cloud credits are managed on our
website only. No purchase is needed to use the local mode, which stays
free and unlimited.

REQUIREMENTS

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). For local mode: an NVIDIA card with at least
8 GB of video memory, 12 GB recommended, and about 25 GB of disk space
for the models. Without an NVIDIA card, only the cloud mode is available.

Built with DINOv3 (Meta Platforms). Third-party licences are listed in
the application, under About.
